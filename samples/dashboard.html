Upcoming upgrades

AlpenglowIn development
Consensus overhaul

Replaces TowerBFT and Proof of History voting with Votor and Rotor, targeting finality in roughly 100-150ms instead of ~12s.

Why it matters: Sub-second finality changes what is buildable on-chain: payments and orderbook trading stop needing off-chain confirmation layers.

SIMD-0525Proposed
Solana Improvement Document

Tracked in the SIMD repository. Check the linked repo for the current stage — SIMDs move between draft, review, accepted and activated.

Why it matters: SIMDs are where protocol-level economics and validator requirements are decided before they reach mainnet.

FiredancerRolling out
Independent validator client

A second full validator implementation written from scratch by Jump Crypto, running alongside Agave.

Why it matters: Client diversity is the single biggest structural risk reducer for a chain: one implementation bug can no longer halt the network.

Token Extensions (Token-2022)Live, adoption ongoing
Token standard

Adds transfer hooks, confidential transfers, interest-bearing tokens and metadata directly at the token program level.

Why it matters: Compliance features at the protocol layer are what institutional and RWA issuers have been waiting for.
